01 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MindWave AI ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Tattoo AI: Design, Font & Stencil iOS app and this website (the "Service"). We are committed to handling your data — especially the photos you upload — with care and transparency.

02 Information We Collect

  • Content you provide: text prompts, reference images, sketches and photos of your body or existing tattoos that you choose to upload.
  • Purchase information: subscription status and transaction identifiers provided by Apple. We never receive your full payment card details.
  • Device & usage data: device model, iOS version, app version, language, crash logs and anonymous interaction events used to improve stability and performance.
  • Advertising identifiers: if you allow tracking through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, we and our ad partners may use your device's advertising identifier. You can change this anytime in Settings.
  • Support communications: your name, email address and message when you contact us.

We do not require you to create an account and we do not collect your contacts, precise location or health data.

03 AI Content Processing

To generate tattoo designs, try-on previews, retouches and meaning explanations, your prompts and images are sent securely to our servers and/or trusted AI processing providers. Processing is performed automatically and only to return the result you requested. Outputs are delivered back to your device.

04 Media Uploads

The app accesses your camera or photo library only when you choose a photo, and only the photos you select are uploaded. Uploaded media and generated content are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours of creation, unless longer retention is strictly necessary for security, fraud prevention or to comply with legal obligations. Designs you save remain on your device under your control.

05 User Consent for AI Processing

Before your first upload, we ask for your consent to process your images with AI. You may withdraw consent at any time by not using the upload features or by contacting us.

5.1 Face Data Collection and Privacy

Photos you upload may incidentally contain your face. When this happens:

  • Facial information ("Face Data") is processed only transiently to render your result (for example, to correctly position a tattoo preview). We do not create facial recognition templates or identify you.
  • Face Data is deleted along with the uploaded image within approximately 24 hours.
  • Face Data is never sold, shared with third parties for their own purposes, or used to train public AI models.

5.2 Body Scan Data and Privacy

The virtual try-on and retouch features analyze photos of body areas (such as your arm, back or leg) to place or remove tattoo artwork realistically.

  • Body images are used solely to generate the preview you requested.
  • They are deleted from our servers within approximately 24 hours and are not used to train public models.
  • Please do not upload nude or intimate images; such content is prohibited.

06 How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Service and its AI features;
  • To process and verify subscriptions and restore purchases;
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs and improve performance;
  • To display advertising in the free version (personalized only with your permission);
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback;
  • To detect abuse and comply with legal obligations.

07 Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with:

  • Service providers (cloud hosting, AI processing, analytics, crash reporting) who process data on our behalf under confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
  • Apple, for payments and subscription management;
  • Advertising partners, limited to device and ad identifiers as permitted by your tracking settings;
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect rights, safety and security.

08 Data Retention

Uploaded media and AI outputs: deleted within 24 hours. Support emails: kept for as long as needed to resolve your request and up to 12 months after. Anonymous analytics and crash data: kept in aggregated form for up to 24 months. Purchase records: retained as required by tax and accounting laws.

09 Data Deletion Requests

You can delete designs saved on your device at any time by deleting them in the app or uninstalling it. To request deletion of any data we may hold about you, email jobertsonrule56425@gmail.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will respond within 30 days.

10 Security

We use industry-standard safeguards, including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS), access controls and automatic deletion of uploaded media. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your information.

11 Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

12 Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

13 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be notified in the app.

14 Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, contact us at jobertsonrule56425@gmail.com or through our contact page.
