FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is Tattoo AI free to use?

Tattoo AI is free to download and contains ads. Some premium features are available through an optional in-app subscription. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and are billed through your Apple ID.

Which devices are supported?

Tattoo AI runs on iPhone with iOS 15.6 or later.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photos are only used to deliver the feature you requested (e.g. try-on or tattoo analysis). Uploaded media and generated content are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I bring the design to my tattoo artist?

Yes! Save or download the design and share it with your artist as a reference for your next tattoo.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Tattoo AI and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.

How can I share feedback?

Tattoo AI always needs your recommendations and feedback to be immensely improved. We'd love to receive your suggestions – send them via our contact page. Thank you very much ❤️
