FAQ

Questions? Answers.

Is Tattoo AI free to use?

Tattoo AI is free to download. Some premium features are available through an optional in-app subscription (Weekly Access). Prices are shown in the app before you buy and are billed through your Apple ID.

Which devices are supported?

Tattoo AI runs on iPhone with iOS 15.6 or later.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photos are only used to deliver the feature you requested (e.g. try-on or meaning analysis). Uploaded media and generated content are automatically deleted from our servers within 24 hours. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I bring the design to my tattoo artist?

Yes! Save the design to your gallery or Photos and share it with your artist. Use the stencil-style output for clean line art references.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Tattoo AI and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal.
